Mr. Pal is a Ph.D. student in Civil Engineering with a focus on Construction Management at the University of Wyoming, USA. He conducts research in the Construction Innovation and Research Lab under the supervision of Prof. Charlie Zhang. His research focuses on energy infrastructure resilience, construction safety, and ergonomics, with an emphasis on the use of physiological sensors such as electroencephalography and eye tracking, along with virtual reality and data-driven methods including artificial intelligence and machine learning.

He holds a bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from Tribhuvan University, Institute of Engineering, Pulchowk Campus, Nepal. Prior to joining the Ph.D. program, he gained professional experience working with government agencies and private sector contractors on building construction and highway and pavement engineering projects.

## Research Interests
- Construction safety and ergonomics  
- Cognitive and human-centered approaches in construction engineering  
- Energy infrastructure resilience  
- Physiological sensing in construction safety research (EEG, eye tracking)  
- Data-driven and machine learning–based safety analysis  

## Current Research
Mr. Pal’s doctoral research investigates construction safety and human factors through controlled experimental and data-driven approaches. His work focuses on understanding cognitive workload, attention, and decision-making in safety-critical construction environments. He employs physiological sensing techniques, including electroencephalography and eye tracking, integrated with virtual reality–based experiments to study worker behavior. The broader goal of this research is to inform the design of safer construction systems and improve resilience in energy and infrastructure projects.
