I recently attended the 2026 LADR Workshop at the CI & CRC Joint Conference in San Antonio, organized by ASCE’s Construction Institute.

During the conference, two of our co-authored papers were presented. Our paper, **"Legal Risk Mitigation in Hydrogen Pipeline Transport Through Corrective Action Order Analysis,"** was presented by my co-author Saksham Timalsina. Another paper, **"Identifying and Reducing Legal Issues in Construction Submittals through Case Law Analysis,"** was presented by Merina Giri.

Both papers focus on understanding legal risks in construction and finding ways to reduce disputes using real-world case data. It was great to see our work being shared and discussed in that setting.

Beyond the presentations, the conference was a really good opportunity to meet people working in construction law and project management. I had several meaningful conversations, made new connections, and also got to reconnect with a few friends from my undergraduate days in Nepal, which made the experience even more special.

Outside the conference, I explored San Antonio, including a downtown tour and a visit to SeaWorld. I also took some time to visit Austin, where I walked around downtown and hiked up Mount Bonnell. The view from the top was definitely worth it.

Overall, this trip was a great mix of research, networking, and a bit of travel. I came back with new ideas, new connections, and a lot of motivation for the next steps in my work.
