I had the opportunity to attend the **AI Unlocked: Empowering Higher Education through Research and Discovery** workshop, representing the Construction Research & Innovation Lab at the University of Wyoming. The event was organized by the NAIRR Pilot (ACCESS Project) in collaboration with the University of Colorado Boulder and was held at The Westin Denver Downtown on April 2–3, 2025.

The workshop provided valuable insights into the fundamentals of artificial intelligence, hands-on experience with advanced AI tools, and guidance on customizing AI models for specific research applications. Sessions also highlighted the use of high-performance computing (HPC) resources to support data-intensive research and model development.

The discussions emphasized how AI and advanced computing resources can accelerate research across disciplines, including engineering, infrastructure systems, and technology-enabled decision-making.

I am grateful to the organizers for creating a collaborative and engaging environment that brought together researchers from diverse fields. The workshop offered both technical learning and meaningful networking opportunities.

In addition to the academic sessions, I appreciated the vibrant atmosphere of downtown Denver and the excellent experience at The Westin Hotel.
