I had the opportunity to present our paper, *“Comparative Analysis of Regulatory Compliance and Legal Issues in Small Modular Nuclear Reactor Deployment,”* at CI Summit 2025, organized by the Construction Institute (CI) of ASCE.

The presentation focused on how regulatory frameworks and legal structures influence the deployment of Small Modular Reactors across different countries. The session sparked meaningful discussions about policy alignment, licensing challenges, and the broader role of regulation in advancing clean energy technologies.

CI Summit provided a valuable platform to exchange ideas with researchers, industry professionals, and academic leaders from various institutions. Engaging with participants from diverse backgrounds strengthened the relevance of our work and highlighted the interdisciplinary nature of nuclear infrastructure development.

I am grateful to the organizers for the opportunity, to my co-authors for their collaboration, and to my advisor for continuous guidance and support. Conferences like this reinforce the importance of dialogue, collaboration, and critical discussion in advancing research.
