I am grateful to have received the 2024 ASTM International Project Grant in support of our ongoing research.

This grant provides valuable support that allows us to further develop our project and strengthen its technical foundation. It also offers the opportunity to share our findings through the ASTM platform, making the work accessible to a broad audience of professionals, researchers, and industry practitioners.

Receiving this support reinforces the importance of research that connects academic investigation with real-world standards and professional practice. I am especially thankful to my advisor, Charlie (Chengyi) Zhang, Ph.D., P.E., for his continued guidance and mentorship throughout this process.

Support from organizations like ASTM plays an important role in advancing research that contributes to both academic knowledge and industry implementation.
