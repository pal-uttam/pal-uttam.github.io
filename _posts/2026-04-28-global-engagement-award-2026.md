I was honored to be recognized as a finalist for the Excellence in Global Engagement Student Award at the University of Wyoming on April 28, 2026.

The award recognizes students, faculty, and staff whose work contributes to global engagement through research, teaching, service, and community involvement.

Being nominated and selected as a finalist means a lot to me. I am grateful to the people who supported and encouraged me throughout this journey.

My work has focused on research, community engagement, and activities that connect people from different backgrounds and experiences. It was meaningful to see these efforts recognized at the university level.

I would like to sincerely thank my nominators, mentors, colleagues, and friends for their continued support and encouragement.

This recognition motivates me to continue working in ways that contribute positively to both local and global communities.
