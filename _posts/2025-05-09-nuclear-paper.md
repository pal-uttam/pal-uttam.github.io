I am pleased to share our recent publication in the *Journal of Legal Affairs and Dispute Resolution in Engineering and Construction* (ASCE).

The article, titled **"Comparative Analysis of Regulatory Compliance and Legal Issues in Small Modular Nuclear Reactor Deployment,"** examines how legal and regulatory practices in different countries influence the deployment of Small Modular Reactors (SMRs). SMRs are increasingly viewed as a promising option for clean and resilient energy systems, particularly in the context of climate and energy security.

The paper discusses how variations in licensing structures, compliance requirements, and legal processes can either support or slow the adoption of this emerging technology. By comparing regulatory approaches, the study highlights key considerations for policymakers and industry stakeholders working toward safe and effective SMR deployment.


[Read the paper on ASCE Library](https://ascelibrary.org/doi/10.1061/JLADAH.LADR-1270){:target="_blank"}
