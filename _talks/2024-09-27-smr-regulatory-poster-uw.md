This poster presented a phase-based safety assessment of micro and small modular reactors (MMRs and SMRs), examining safety considerations across the full project lifecycle, from conceptual design and regulatory approval to construction, operation, and decommissioning.

The study identified phase-specific risk categories, regulatory intersections, and construction-related safety challenges that influence project feasibility and long-term resilience. By structuring safety evaluation across lifecycle stages, the research highlights how early design and licensing decisions shape downstream construction risks and operational reliability.

The work received an Honorable Mention and 4th place award for its interdisciplinary contribution to construction management, energy systems, and infrastructure safety.
